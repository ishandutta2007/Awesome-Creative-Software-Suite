## Top Creative Software Suite
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Graphic Design, Illustration, Photo Editing, UI/UX, 3D, Video & Full Creative Toolchains*

**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Creative Software Suites**. These products help designers, illustrators, photographers, 3D artists, video editors, and creative teams produce professional visual work across raster, vector, UI, photo, 3D, and motion pipelines.

**Examples** include Adobe Creative Cloud, CorelDRAW Graphics Suite, Canva Pro, Figma, Sketch, Affinity Designer, Pixlr, CyberLink Director Suite, Autodesk Creative tools, and ACDSee Photo Studio Ultimate (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: The creative software space has one of the strongest open ecosystems in software. **GIMP**, **Inkscape**, **Krita**, **Penpot**, **Blender**, **darktable**, **Scribus**, **Kdenlive**, and related projects deliver production-ready alternatives that are free, transparent, and self-hostable or fully offline. This section is heavily expanded.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Adobe Creative Cloud](https://www.adobe.com/creativecloud.html)**  
  Industry-standard suite covering Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, InDesign, Lightroom, and more with deep integration and cloud services.

- **[CorelDRAW Graphics Suite](https://www.coreldraw.com/)**  
  Professional vector illustration, layout, and photo editing suite long popular for print, signage, and design workflows.

- **[Canva Pro](https://www.canva.com/)**  
  Accessible browser-based design platform with templates, brand kits, collaboration, and AI features for teams and individuals.

- **[Figma](https://www.figma.com/)**  
  Leading collaborative UI/UX design and prototyping tool with real-time multiplayer editing, design systems, and developer handoff.

- **[Sketch](https://www.sketch.com/)**  
  Mac-native vector design tool focused on interface design, symbols, and a large plugin ecosystem.

- **[Affinity Designer](https://affinity.serif.com/en-us/designer/)**  
  High-performance vector and raster design application (part of the Affinity suite) known for one-time purchase model and professional feature set.

- **[Pixlr](https://pixlr.com/)**  
  Browser and mobile photo editor and design tool with layers, AI features, and quick creative workflows.

- **[CyberLink Director Suite](https://www.cyberlink.com/)**  
  Consumer-to-pro video editing, color grading, audio, and disc authoring suite with AI-assisted tools.

- **[Autodesk Creative Tools](https://www.autodesk.com/)**  
  Professional 3D modeling, animation, and visualization applications including Maya, 3ds Max, and related creative pipelines.

- **[ACDSee Photo Studio Ultimate](https://www.acdsee.com/)**  
  All-in-one photo management, RAW processing, and advanced editing application aimed at photographers.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Penpot](https://github.com/penpot/penpot)**  
  Fully open-source design and prototyping platform (MPL-2.0). Real-time collaboration, design tokens, CSS/SVG output, and self-hostable — the strongest open alternative to Figma.

- **[GIMP](https://github.com/GNOME/gimp)**  
  Mature raster image editor for photo retouching, compositing, and graphic design. The classic open-source counterpart to Photoshop.

- **[Inkscape](https://gitlab.com/inkscape/inkscape)**  
  Professional vector graphics editor built around SVG. Excellent for logos, illustrations, icons, and print work — primary open alternative to Illustrator and CorelDRAW.

- **[Krita](https://github.com/KDE/krita)**  
  Digital painting and illustration powerhouse with an outstanding brush engine, animation tools, and comic workflow features.

- **[Blender](https://github.com/blender/blender)**  
  Complete 3D creation suite covering modeling, sculpting, animation, simulation, rendering, compositing, and video editing. Industry-grade and fully open.

- **[darktable](https://github.com/darktable-org/darktable)**  
  Non-destructive RAW photo workflow and library manager. The leading open-source alternative to Adobe Lightroom.

- **[Scribus](https://github.com/scribusproject/scribus)**  
  Desktop publishing application for multi-page layouts, magazines, books, and professional print PDF production (InDesign alternative).

- **[Kdenlive](https://invent.kde.org/multimedia/kdenlive)**  
  Powerful multi-track video editor with effects, transitions, and timeline tools. Strong open-source option for video production.

- **[RawTherapee](https://github.com/Beep6581/RawTherapee)**  
  Advanced RAW converter and photo processor focused on high-quality image development and fine control.

- **[MyPaint](https://github.com/mypaint/mypaint)**  
  Fast, distraction-free digital painting application with natural brushes, ideal for sketching and illustration.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Full creative stacks: Combine **GIMP + Inkscape + Krita + Penpot + Blender + darktable + Scribus + Kdenlive**.
- UI/UX & product design: **Penpot** (self-hostable or hosted).
- Photo & raster: **GIMP**, **darktable**, **RawTherapee**, **Pinta**.
- Vector & illustration: **Inkscape**, **Krita**.
- 3D, motion & VFX: **Blender**.
- Video editing: **Kdenlive**, **Shotcut**, **OpenShot**.
- Page layout & print: **Scribus**, **LibreOffice Draw**.
- Emerging / specialized: Graphite (vector), Excalidraw / tldraw (diagrams), ArtCraft suite (experimental Rust Adobe-style apps).
- Accepting that commercial suites (Adobe Creative Cloud, Figma, Affinity, CorelDRAW, Canva) still lead in polish, ecosystem integrations, support, and certain specialized pro features.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Assemble a complete free creative workstation with **GIMP** (raster) + **Inkscape** (vector) + **Krita** (painting) + **Penpot** (UI) + **Blender** (3D/motion) + **darktable** (photo) + **Scribus** (layout) + **Kdenlive** (video). Deploy on any OS or use distributions such as Ubuntu Studio for a ready-made creative environment. Ideal for individuals, studios, and organizations that want zero subscription cost, full data ownership, and the ability to audit or extend the tools.
